About Me

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics at the University of Kansas.

My research interests primarily focus on Commutative Algebra, with a sustained interest in advanced mathematics, including Lie Algebras, homological algebra, and Galois theory.

Prior to this, I completed my Master of Science in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, and my Bachelor of Science in Mathematics at St. Xavier's College, Kolkata.
