Hello, I’m

Soumyadeep Misra

I am Soumyadeep Misra, a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of Kansas, working under the guidance of Prof. Hai Long Dao. I am interested in commutative algebra and its interactions with combinatorics, especially Stanley–Reisner theory and graph-theoretic aspects. I am currently serving as Treasurer for the Graduate Student Organization in the Department of Mathematics. Prior to this, I completed my Master of Science in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, and my Bachelor of Science in Mathematics at St Xavier's College, Kolkata.

📍 Lawrence, Kansas ✉️ soumyadeep@ku.edu
