Overview

Broadly, my research interests lie in Commutative Algebra.

Working Papers & Current Projects

Currently, my focus is directed toward the interactions between commutative algebra and combinatorics. Specific areas of active exploration include:

  • Stanley–Reisner theory and its applications.
  • Graph-theoretic aspects and properties of edge ideals.
