Research

Overview

Broadly, my research interests lie in Commutative Algebra.My research is in commutative algebra, with a focus on the algebraic and combinatorial properties of ideals and rings. I am particularly interested in the regularity of edge ideals, the behavior of symbolic powers, and the study of Hilbert functions and vectors of Stanley–Reisner rings.
