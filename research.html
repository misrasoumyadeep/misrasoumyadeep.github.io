Research program

Research

My interests lie in commutative algebra and its interactions with combinatorics, with emphasis on Stanley–Reisner theory, graph-theoretic aspects of ideals, monomial ideals, and symbolic powers.

Overview

Commutative algebra and combinatorial structures

Broadly, I am interested in questions where algebraic invariants of ideals can be studied through combinatorial or geometric models. Current directions include Stanley–Reisner ideals, graph-theoretic constructions, symbolic powers, ordinary powers, containment problems, Serre conditions, and homological invariants of monomial ideals.

Representative objects include $S/I$, symbolic powers $I^{(m)}$, ordinary powers $I^r$, edge ideals, simplicial complexes, and invariants such as multiplicity, regularity, codimension, and depth.
